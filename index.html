Advisory

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), free for non-commercial use. Block points are approximate centres and the data grid is about 10 km, so neighbouring blocks can look alike. How it works: each block runs 400 simulated 6-week rainfall seasons (wet/dry-day Markov chain). ENSO, IOD, MJO and recent rain shift the odds. Onset = 30 mm in 3 days; dry spell = 5+ dry days in a week; false onset = 7+ dry days within 14 days of onset; heavy = a day of 40 mm or more. Block climatology and index effects are illustrative, not yet calibrated on IMD data.
